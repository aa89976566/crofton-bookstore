How to reserve

There is no checkout and no account. You browse, hold, and email. We confirm what we can keep aside for you.

  1. Browse the online shelf. Only a slice of the shop is listed. Stock turns quickly.
  2. Open a title to read condition notes, binding, edition details and availability.
  3. Add titles to your reserve list, then email us. We confirm what we can hold.
  4. Collect in Brockley, or ask about postage when you write. No online payment and no account.

About

Crofton Books opened in Brockley in 2021, founded by local poet and writer Jason Shelley with a team of fellow book addicts.

We sell a mix of secondhand, antiquarian and rare books alongside trending new literary titles. Expect colour shelves, hand drawn floor plans, and stacks that spill onto the pavement.

Come for the Penguin classics and Occult curiosities. Stay for the armchairs, book clubs, poetry nights, and the blackboard questions outside.

Owner: Jason Shelley. Est. 2021. Class free browsing, creative displays, and curated collections that feel as much museum as shop.

Condition guide

Secondhand books are described honestly. Grades follow common antiquarian practice. Always read the notes on each title.

New
Unread or as issued. May still carry shop or publisher marks.
Near fine
Minimal wear. Clean text block, sound binding, only light shelf rubbing.
Very good
Gently used. Small marks or edge wear possible. No major faults.
Good
Honestly used and fully readable. May show creases, fading or owner names.
Reading copy
Worn but intact for reading. Priced for use, not for the collector shelf.
